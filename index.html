About Our Practice

A Family Tradition of Dental Excellence

Hohman Family Dentistry P.C. has been a cornerstone of the Holdrege community for decades. Founded by Dr. Doug Hohman DDS, the practice now proudly carries forward with Dr. Isaac Hohman DDS, bringing two generations of expertise under one roof.

We believe great dental care starts with listening — understanding your concerns, goals, and comfort level to deliver personalized treatment that puts your health and confidence first.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Dental Services

Preventive Care

Regular cleanings, exams, X-rays, and fluoride treatments to keep your teeth healthy and catch issues early.

Restorative Dentistry

Fillings, crowns, bridges, and implants to restore the function and beauty of damaged or missing teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and smile makeovers designed to give you the confidence you deserve.

Pediatric Dentistry

Kid-friendly dental care in a warm, welcoming environment — building healthy habits from the very first visit.

Emergency Dentistry

Same-day appointments for toothaches, broken teeth, and dental emergencies — when you need us most.

Dentures & Implants

Full and partial dentures and dental implants to restore your smile's function and aesthetics for life.
